Mission Statement

As UG Representative, I want every UG resident to feel heard, and every College team to play an equal part in building our community.

Over two years on the Event Management Team, I helped run High Table Dinners, Mid-Autumn Festival, Halloween, Valentine’s Day and Superpass. I’ve also trained with the SHC dragon boat team since first year and raced the short course at Stanley in 2026.

This year, I’m on the Student Committee leading Newcomers’ Reception and other community building projects, and as a Resident Student Advisor I do my part to help residents settle in. As Discovery Club PIC, I take residents out to explore Hong Kong’s neighborhoods, art and cultures.

If elected, I will bring residents’ ideas and concerns to the tutors and keep everyone updated on what comes of them. I will also start an SHC alumni network, so our ties to the College and to each other outlast our years here.

Shun Hing welcomed me as a newcomer, and it has been home ever since. I would be grateful for the chance to give back, so that no one here has to figure out university, Hong Kong or growing up on their own.

Thank you for your support!
